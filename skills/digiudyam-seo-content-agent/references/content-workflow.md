# Content, E-E-A-T, GEO, metadata, schema, and images

Read this reference for content briefs, drafting, optimization, or page-level SEO recommendations.

## Research and brief

Analyze topic, intent, questions, objections, SERP patterns, required depth, related entities, and information gain. Classify material claims as verified source, user-approved business fact, inference, or unsupported.

Create a brief with audience, purpose, conversion goal, angle, URL, one descriptive H1, logical H2/H3 hierarchy, evidence needed, internal links, CTA, metadata, schema, and image needs. Do not target an arbitrary word count.

## People-first writing

Answer the core question early. Add context, choices, process, tradeoffs, limitations, and next steps. Use simple professional Indian English unless another language is requested. Explain necessary terms plainly.

Keep headings semantic; do not turn body copy into oversized headings. Use lists and tables only when they make decisions easier.

## E-E-A-T

- **Experience:** use genuine supplied or documented processes, examples, screenshots, and outcomes. Request missing proof rather than simulating it.
- **Expertise:** explain requirements, tradeoffs, limitations, and next steps accurately.
- **Authority:** connect the topic to verified DigiUdyam services and use primary sources for external facts.
- **Trust:** provide accurate authorship/company context, contact and policy links, transparent approved pricing, and qualified claims.

Recommend real trust inputs when missing: author or reviewer details, process artefacts, project screenshots, scope, ownership terms, policies, and genuine case evidence.

## Generative discovery

There is no guaranteed GEO formula. Give direct answers, state entities and relationships clearly, use coherent sections and non-repetitive FAQs, attribute factual claims, and keep key information visible rather than only in schema.

## Metadata

Provide a unique truthful SEO title, meta description, canonical URL, Open Graph data, and Twitter card data. Align them with visible content and dominant intent. Avoid stuffing and unsupported promises.

## Schema

Use only schema supported by verified visible content: Organization, genuine LocalBusiness, Service, BreadcrumbList, appropriate FAQPage, and genuine Article data.

Never add fake ratings, reviews, addresses, authors, prices, service areas, or affiliations. Validate JSON-LD and page/schema consistency. Rich results are not guaranteed.

## Image SEO

For each meaningful image define purpose, source or proof value, filename, dimensions, alt text, and priority.

- Prefer original work, real process images, accurate screenshots, and licensed relevant imagery.
- Use empty alt text for decorative images.
- Describe function and context, not keywords.
- Use Next.js Image where appropriate, reserve dimensions, prioritize only likely LCP imagery, and lazy-load below the fold.
- Do not add stock images merely to meet an image count.
