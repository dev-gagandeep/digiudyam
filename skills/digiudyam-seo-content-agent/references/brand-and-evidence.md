# DigiUdyam brand and evidence rules

## Verified baseline supplied for this agent

DigiUdyam is a private digital growth company helping Indian MSMEs, entrepreneurs, and local businesses with website development, local SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, digital marketing, CRM, and automation.

Write primarily for Indian business owners who want clearer online visibility, customer enquiries, lead management, or simpler workflows.

## Voice

- Professional, simple, friendly, and practical
- Business-outcome language before technical terminology
- Confident without hype
- Helpful consultant, not a generic agency salesperson
- Indian English by default; use Hindi or Hinglish only when requested

Prefer concrete phrases such as “help customers find your business” and “manage enquiries and follow-ups.” Avoid inflated phrases such as “revolutionary digital ecosystem” unless they appear in user-approved copy that must remain unchanged.

## Claims boundary

The baseline does not prove a physical office, service area, founding date, founder identity, team size, credentials, awards, partnerships, Udyam registration, clients, project counts, ratings, testimonials, case studies, measured results, prices, timelines, guarantees, or proprietary methods.

Obtain these facts from the user or a verifiable first-party source before publishing them. DigiUdyam's name and audience do not imply affiliation with the Government of India or the Udyam registration portal. Do not imply such an affiliation.

## Evidence hierarchy

Use sources in this order:

1. User-approved DigiUdyam materials and first-party business records
2. Official government, platform, standards, or product documentation
3. Original research and reputable industry sources
4. Credible secondary reporting when primary evidence is unavailable

For every consequential fact, retain the source URL, publication or update date when available, access date, and exact proposition it supports. Separate source facts from analysis. A search snippet alone is not sufficient evidence.

For service content, request genuine process details, deliverables, limitations, author or reviewer information, and real proof where useful. If unavailable, create an input checklist rather than fictional experience.
