# Approval, deliverable, and reporting format

Adapt output to the requested mode. Do not bury the primary deliverable under process notes.

## Strategy or SEO analysis

- Opportunity and business relevance
- Research query, location, language, and date
- Intent, user problems, SERP pattern, and content gap
- Existing-site overlap and cannibalization assessment
- Decision: improve, create, merge, retain, or change URL
- Proposed URL and cluster placement
- Evidence, assumptions, risks, and inputs needed

## Content review package

- SEO brief
- Clean draft separated from notes
- Metadata and canonical
- Schema recommendation
- Incoming and outgoing links
- Image plan
- Evidence ledger and unresolved claims
- Verdict: `APPROVED`, `APPROVED WITH INPUTS NEEDED`, or `NOT READY`

If review is required before implementation, stop and request approval.

## Implementation report

- SEO opportunity
- Keyword target and intent
- URL decision and cluster placement
- Pages created, updated, merged, or redirected
- Exact files changed
- Links added or updated
- Metadata and canonical changes
- Schema and breadcrumb changes
- Sitemap, robots, redirect, or other technical changes
- Images added or optimized
- Content fidelity to approved draft
- TypeScript, build, route, browser, and SEO validation results
- Remaining risks or manual follow-ups

## Audit result

Score applicable areas pass, revise, blocked, or not applicable: intent and business value; architecture and cannibalization; usefulness and originality; E-E-A-T; generative clarity; headings; metadata; canonical; schema; internal linking; image SEO; crawlability; indexability; sitemap; robots; redirects; responsive experience; build safety.

End with `SEO VALIDATION: PASSED`, `SEO VALIDATION: PASSED WITH FOLLOW-UPS`, or `SEO VALIDATION: FAILED`.
